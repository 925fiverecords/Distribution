About 925five Records

Independent music. Long-term development.

925five Records Distribution Management is an independent entertainment company founded on October 21, 2014 by music entrepreneur Lovie Johnson. Built on a commitment to creative independence and sustainable artist careers, the company provides management, distribution, branding, marketing strategy, and professional development services.

During its early development, Johnson assembled a team of radio promotion and marketing personnel while personally overseeing the company’s publishing and artist management efforts. Johnson invested approximately $1 million and a year of development in establishing a headquarters in Bakersfield, California, with three recording studios and administrative office space.

925five Records emphasizes discovering emerging artists and building their audiences. The company uses streaming analytics, online audience insights, and market research to identify the regions where each artist is gaining traction, supporting informed promotional strategies.

925five Records distribution

Your sound. A clear strategy. Personal label support.

925five Records operates as a selective music distribution partner within the EMPIRE distribution network. Through its distribution arrangements, the company assists approved projects with delivery preparation, including master audio, cover artwork, metadata, credits, and release information.

Applications are reviewed for music quality, rights eligibility, professionalism, and overall fit. Access to the 90/10 distribution program is currently by invitation only. Submission does not guarantee acceptance.
